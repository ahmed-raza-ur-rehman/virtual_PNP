# Feature Tradeoffs

| Profile | Speed | Accuracy | Use case |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | medium | medium | general SMD |
| High-Speed | high | lower | high volume, large components |
| High-Accuracy | low | high | fine-pitch, BGA, 0201 |
| Multi-Head x4 | high | medium | parallel placement, throughput |

FPGA vs MCU: FPGA gives deterministic sub-microsecond timing; MCU cheaper/flexible.
ASIC: lowest unit cost at volume, highest NRE.
